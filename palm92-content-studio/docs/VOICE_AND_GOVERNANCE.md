# Faith and Palm92 voice guide

## Voice

- Write like Faith speaks: warm, clear, direct, reflective and practical.
- Use a real moment or lived lesson when Faith has supplied one. Never invent a personal anecdote.
- Explain technical ideas in plain English without talking down to readers.
- Use short paragraphs, a strong first line, one central point, and a natural discussion question.
- Keep Palm92's principle visible where relevant: **Human judgement → AI acceleration → Human verification → Learning.**
- Avoid empty hype, robotic phrasing, excessive claims, and em dashes.

## Editorial checks

1. Separate verified facts from Faith's interpretation and from any personal story.
2. Verify current claims and cite sources in the post when needed.
3. Mark unclear details `[VERIFY]`; never fill gaps by guessing.
4. Never borrow another creator's voice, story, identity, visuals or growth results.
5. Do not reveal private family, health, immigration, employment or beneficiary information without specific approval.
6. Give one post one job: teach, tell a story, show progress, invite discussion, or make a clear offer.
7. Use accessible visuals: readable contrast, simple composition, accurate spelling and captions for video.

## Approval lifecycle

`Captured → Draft → Needs edit → Approved → Scheduled/Published → Measured`

Only Faith can move work to `Approved`. Scheduling or publishing requires approval of the exact post, visual, platform, date and time. The MVP does not publish automatically.

## Metrics

Track reach, relevant comments, saves/shares, profile visits and meaningful enquiries. Compare each post with the account's own recent baseline. Do not present a creator's follower-growth claim as a forecast for Faith.
