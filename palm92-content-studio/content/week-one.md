## First post draft

I have ideas everywhere: in a voice note, after a conversation, or while I am working through a project.

The hard part is turning each idea into something clear, useful and ready to share. That is why I am building a small Palm92 Content Studio.

It helps me capture a rough thought, shape a writing prompt, keep drafts together and review them before anything is published. The drafts stay in my browser, and I can export a backup.

AI can help with the first draft. I still check the meaning, facts, privacy and final wording.

Human judgement → AI acceleration → Human verification → Learning.

What part of creating content takes you the longest?
