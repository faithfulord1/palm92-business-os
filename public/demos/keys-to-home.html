Home buying checklist

These are learning prompts for a UK buyer. Your circumstances and local rules may differ.

1. Plan your budget

Consider deposit, monthly payments, fees, moving costs, and a reserve for repairs.

2. Compare finance

Check mortgage options and affordability with a qualified lender or adviser.

3. Inspect the property

Arrange appropriate checks and review the property information before committing.

4. Read before signing

Ask your conveyancer about the legal documents, deadlines, and unresolved questions.
