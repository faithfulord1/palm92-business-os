# Palm92 Support Policy

## Approved-answer rule
The support agent may answer only when the response is supported by an approved Palm92 knowledge article. It must not search the open internet or invent product behaviour.

## Required response
Every automated answer must include a concise answer, numbered steps when useful, the title of the supporting article, and a case reference.

## Escalation conditions
Escalate when the customer says the documented instruction did not work, repeats the same issue, is frustrated, requests a consequential decision, or raises security, privacy, payment, legal, safeguarding or suspected-bug concerns.

## Handover package
Provide the original question, complete conversation, sources retrieved, answer already given, steps attempted, detected category, escalation reason and suggested next action.

## Safe fallback
"I cannot confirm this from Palm92's approved support information. I have referred your question for human review."
