# Faithfulord Affiliate Studio

A standalone, UK-first campaign workspace inspired by the process in Ramon Martins’s Shopee affiliate video. It supports product research, original content drafts, an affiliate disclosure gate, an opt-in lead flow, campaign planning, performance logging and a dependency checklist.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a current browser. There is no installation, server, account, paid API key or package dependency. The app stores records in that browser’s local storage. Use **Data & settings → Export backup** before moving to another device.

## Current scope

The app prepares and tracks work. It does not log into Instagram, publish posts, send direct messages, create affiliate links, scrape products, or enrol anyone in a WhatsApp group. Those actions need the relevant account, programme acceptance and approved permissions. Until configured, use the manual steps in the Dependencies and Lead Flow screens.

## UK programme options to check

Requirements change, so check the official terms before applying or posting.

- TikTok Shop UK creator page: https://business.tiktokshop.com/gb/creator
- Amazon Associates UK review and social page requirements: https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM
- eBay Partner Network UK: https://partnernetwork.ebay.co.uk/
- ASA guidance on recognising affiliate ads on social media: https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/recognising-ads-social-media.html

At the time this workspace was prepared, TikTok Shop’s UK creator page listed 18+ and 500 followers. Amazon UK’s help page said social pages generally need at least 500 organic followers and 10 original posts, then three qualifying sales within 180 days for review. These are screening facts to re-check, not a promise of acceptance.

## Safe campaign path

1. Choose a programme that accepts your account and UK audience.
2. Save a real product listing, tracked link and seller/evidence notes.
3. Create an original concept from verified facts. Do not copy a creator’s caption, image, voice, identity or protected assets.
4. Put “Ad” clearly at the beginning of content where you earn commission. Do not claim personal use or product results unless true and supported.
5. Invite people to request a link. Follow up only as they opted in. Prefer a WhatsApp Channel for deal updates so members do not see each other’s phone numbers.
6. Record views, clicks, orders, cancellations/returns and commission separately. Commission is not profit, and views do not guarantee sales.

## Privacy and data

All entered product, campaign and performance data stays in local browser storage until the user exports or clears it. Do not enter passwords, bank details, private customer information or platform access tokens.
